# DCMS voluntary code checklist (template)

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have your compliance reviewed by a qualified solicitor if you can.

The Department for Culture, Media and Sport's
[Voluntary Code of Good Practice for Prize Draw Operators](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/voluntary-code-of-good-practice-for-prize-draw-operators/voluntary-code-of-good-practice-for-prize-draw-operators)
was checked for this checklist on 13 November 2026, when the page said "Updated 19 October 2026". Clause numbers below
are from that version. **Read the current gov.uk text before relying on this list**: the code can change. The exact
words of the clauses the template meets are quoted in clause 15 of [content/terms.md](../../content/terms.md).

To sign the code, email the address the code gives (prizedrawcode@dcms.gov.uk). FairDraw's rubric checks the gov.uk
signatory list; set `voluntaryCodeSignatory` to `true` in `config/site.json` once you are on it.

| Clause | Subject (summary, not the code's words) | What the template does | What you must do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | Players 18 or over; age verification; no advertising aimed at under-18s | Date of birth checked at sign-up and every checkout; hook for a third-party verifier (docs/age-verification.md) | Decide whether self-declared date of birth is a "reasonable" process for you; never target ads at under-18s |
| 1.2 | Complaints process and dispute resolution | Complaints clause in the terms; [complaints.md](complaints.md) | Run the process; choose a dispute resolution route |
| 1.3 | Credit card limits (£250 a month; none for instant wins) | **Not enforced** | Configure your payment provider before you claim it |
| 1.4 | Monthly spend limits, settable at registration, down to £0 | Daily, weekly and monthly limits, offered at sign-up, down to £0 | Nothing |
| 1.5 | Temporary suspension (at least 6 months) and permanent closure; optional shorter pause; no marketing during suspension | Self-exclusion from 6 months, take-a-break from 24 hours, account deletion, no marketing at all | Nothing |
| 1.6 | Monitoring players for signs of harm | Staff can see limits, breaks and orders in player support | Put a monitoring process in place |
| 1.7 | Tailored intervention where harm is indicated | Not automated | Decide how you intervene and record it |
| 1.8 | Signposting to support services | Responsible play page | Check the support services listed are current |
| 1.9 | Appropriate time between opening and closing | Not enforced | Set sensible dates for every competition |
| 1.10 | Instant win draws: free entry equivalence; not the majority of competitions | No instant wins | If you add them, follow 1.10 and 2.4 |
| 1.11 | Advertising follows CAP and BCAP codes and is socially responsible | Nothing to do with ads | Follow it in all your marketing |
| 2.1 | Clear summary of how each draw is run; "laws of chance" statement | "How our draws work" page; terms clauses 12 and 15 | Nothing |
| 2.2 | Fair award; verifiably random and auditable process; equal chance for free and paid entries; publish the mechanism | Verifiable drand draw with public records and a verify page; one draw for every ticket however entered | Nothing |
| 2.3 | Information about the likelihood of winning | Maximum tickets and tickets sold on every competition | Nothing |
| 2.4 | Free entry route shown clearly before purchase, by ordinary post or an equivalent | Free entry on every competition page and in the terms | Process postal entries promptly and record every one |
| 2.5 | Prompt award of the advertised prize; no smaller prize, no date change, no cancellation for low sales | Hard-wired: drawn at the advertised time for the full prize; terms and times locked once published | Deliver prizes promptly |
| 2.6 | Clear information about charitable contributions | None | Follow it if you make contributions |
| 3.1 | Monitor and review your own compliance | Audit log; this checklist | Review regularly |
| 3.2 | Third parties supporting your draws follow the code | None | Cover it in your contracts |
| 3.3 | Share best practice across the sector | None | Optional |
| 3.4 | Publish your player protection, transparency and accountability measures | Responsible play page, "How our draws work", results and draw records | Keep the pages current |
| 3.5 | Work with DCMS on the code | None | Optional |
