# Sources of the legal and GDPR documents

> **Template, not legal advice.** These documents are built from texts others have published for reuse, adjusted for
> this template. Have them reviewed by a qualified solicitor before you rely on them if you can.

Every legal and GDPR document in this repository, where its text came from, under what licence, when the source was
checked, what was copied word for word, and what was changed and why. All sources were fetched and read on the date
shown; nothing here relies on memory of a source.

**Licences differ.** The template's code is under the CPAL-1.0 (and `src/lib/fairdraw/` under MIT): see
[LICENSE](../../LICENSE), [NOTICE](../../NOTICE) and [licence.md](../licence.md). Documents that adapt Creative Commons
or Open Government Licence material carry that material's licence and attribution, shown on the document itself.
