# Complaints procedure (template)

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have this procedure reviewed by a qualified solicitor before you rely on it.

## How to complain

Email [hello@example.com] (`CONTACT_EMAIL`) with "Complaint" in the subject, or write to [Operator Ltd],
[registered office address]. Tell us your name, the email on your account (if you have one), the competition and what
went wrong.

## What we do

1. We acknowledge your complaint within [2] working days.
2. A member of staff not involved in the matter looks into it. For any complaint about a draw, we check the published
   draw record and re-run the draw; you can do this yourself on the draw's "verify this draw" page.
3. We reply in full within [14] days. If we need longer, we tell you why and when to expect an answer.
4. If you are unhappy with our answer, ask for it to be reviewed by [a director]. We reply within [14] days.
5. If you are still unhappy, you can [describe any independent route your solicitor recommends]. For complaints about
   your personal data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

## Records

We keep a log of complaints (date, summary, outcome, time taken) for [3] years and review it every [quarter] to fix
recurring problems.
