# Responsible play (template)

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have this page reviewed by a qualified solicitor before you rely on it, and check the
> support organisations listed are current.

Competitions should be fun. Only spend what you can afford, and never chase losses.

## Tools in your account

- **Spend limits.** Set a daily, weekly and monthly limit, down to £0. Lowering a limit takes effect immediately.
  Raising or removing one takes effect after 24 hours, and you can cancel the change in that time.
- **Take a break.** Stop entering for 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or 6 weeks. A break cannot be shortened once started.
- **Self-exclusion.** Stop entering for 6 months, 1 year, 2 years or 5 years. It cannot be undone early, and it still
  applies if you delete your account and sign up again.
- **Account history.** Your account lists every order and ticket. You can download all your data at any time.

## 18 or over only

You must be 18 or over to play. We check your date of birth when you sign up and may ask for proof of age before
awarding a prize.

## Signs it may be a problem

Spending more than you meant to, borrowing to play, hiding spending from others, or feeling anxious when you are not
playing. If any of these sound familiar, use the tools above and talk to someone.

## Free, confidential support

These include the services the DCMS Voluntary Code of Good Practice for Prize Draw Operators names in clause 1.8
(checked 13 November 2026; see SOURCES.md). Check each link in a browser before you go live.

- GamCare and the National Gambling Helpline: https://www.gamcare.org.uk
- GambleAware: https://www.gambleaware.org
- Citizens Advice, for money worries: https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/debt-and-money/
- National Debtline, run by the Money Advice Trust: https://nationaldebtline.org
- Samaritans: https://www.samaritans.org
- Mind: https://www.mind.org.uk
