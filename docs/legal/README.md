# Legal templates

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have every document here reviewed by a qualified solicitor before you rely on it if
> you can.

These documents are built from texts others have published for reuse, adjusted for this template. Where each one came
from, its licence and what was changed are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [../../content/terms.md](../../content/terms.md) | The competition terms the site shows at `/terms`: one clause per CAP Code significant condition (rules 8.17 and 8.28), free entry wording following Gambling Commission and CAP guidance, and the DCMS Voluntary Code's commitments quoted exactly |
| [terms-template.md](terms-template.md) | How the terms are built and what you must fill in or decide |
| [dcms-voluntary-code-checklist.md](dcms-voluntary-code-checklist.md) | The DCMS Voluntary Code, clause by clause, with what the template does and what you must do |
| [responsible-play.md](responsible-play.md) | The responsible play page shown to players |
| [complaints.md](complaints.md) | A simple complaints procedure |
| [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) | Provenance of every legal and GDPR document |

Privacy and data protection documents are in [../gdpr/](../gdpr/README.md). The software licence is explained in
[../licence.md](../licence.md).

Your company details, contact addresses and free entry address come from `config/site.json` and fill the
`{{PLACEHOLDERS}}` in `content/terms.md` and `content/privacy.md`.
