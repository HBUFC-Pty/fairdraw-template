# Legal templates

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have every document here reviewed by a qualified solicitor before you rely on it.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [terms-template.md](terms-template.md) | Prize competition terms, written for the Gambling Act 2005 prize competition and free draw exemptions |
| [responsible-play.md](responsible-play.md) | The responsible play page shown to players |
| [dcms-voluntary-code-checklist.md](dcms-voluntary-code-checklist.md) | A checklist for the DCMS voluntary code of good practice for prize draw operators |
| [complaints.md](complaints.md) | A simple complaints procedure |

Privacy and data protection templates are in [../gdpr/](../gdpr/README.md).

The site renders its own terms, privacy and responsible play pages from your settings in `.env` (see
`.env.example`). Keep those pages and these documents in step: if your solicitor changes a clause here, change the
page too.
