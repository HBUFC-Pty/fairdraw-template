# Data subject rights procedure (template)

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have this procedure reviewed by a qualified solicitor or data protection adviser
> before you rely on it.

## Time limit

Respond within one calendar month of receiving the request (or of receiving any ID you reasonably need). You can
extend by up to two further months for complex or numerous requests, telling the person within the first month and
explaining why. Requests are normally free.

## Steps

1. **Log** the request: date received, who, what they ask for, deadline. Requests can arrive by any channel, not only
   the privacy email.
2. **Check identity** proportionately. A request from the account's own email address, or made while logged in, is
   usually enough. Ask for more only if you have real doubt, and do not ask for more than you need.
3. **Find the data.** Players can download a full JSON export themselves at Account, Your data ("Download my data").
   For a request made by email, log in as support, find the user, and send the same export (or ask the person to use the
   button). Also check: postal entries under their email, emails in your mailbox, notes, backups (tell them backups
   expire on the backup schedule), processors.
4. **Other rights.** Correction: edit the record. Deletion: the player can use Account, Delete account, which keeps
   only what the law requires (see `retention.md`); explain what is kept and why. Withdrawal of winner-name consent:
   the player can do it in their account.
5. **Exemptions.** Remove other people's data. Take advice before refusing any part.
6. **Respond** securely, in a commonly used electronic format, and record what was sent and when.
7. **Tell them** they can complain to the ICO: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/
