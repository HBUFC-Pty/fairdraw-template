## About this website

The ICO generator does not cover the points below, so we explain them here.

### Cookies

We use one cookie, and only when you log in. It is called `__Host-fd_session`. It keeps you logged in and holds a
random identifier; our server stores only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it. It lasts 30 days for players, 12 hours for
staff, or until you log out.

This cookie is strictly necessary to give you the account you asked for, so the law does not require us to ask for your
consent, and we do not show a cookie banner. We do not use analytics, advertising or social media cookies, tracking
pixels or anything similar. Our emails are plain text with no tracking. Read our full [cookie statement](/cookies).

### Public entry lists

Every competition has a public list of entries so that anyone can check who could win, and that the list was not changed
after the competition closed. The list shows **ticket numbers and short entry codes only, never names, email addresses
or postal addresses**. Your own entry codes are shown in your account, so you can find your tickets on the list. Nobody
else can tell which tickets are yours.

### Draw records

When a competition is drawn we publish a draw record: the list of ticket numbers, a fingerprint of that list, the public
random number used, and the winning ticket numbers. Draw records contain **no personal information**. We keep them
online permanently, because they are the proof that each draw was fair. The random number comes from the public drand
network; we send drand nothing about you.

### Winners

We contact winners by email or post. On our results pages a winner appears only as a ticket number unless they agree to
show their **first name and the first letter of their surname** (for example "Sam T."). You can give or withdraw that
agreement at any time in your account. We never show full names, addresses or photos without separate, explicit
agreement.

### Age checks

You must be 18 or over to play. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and check it again before you buy
entries. We use it only to check your age and keep it while your account is open. [If you add an age verification

### Responsible play tools

If you set spend limits, take a break or self-exclude, we keep those settings to apply them. If you self-exclude and
then delete your account, we keep a one-way scrambled fingerprint of your email address (not the address itself) until
the exclusion ends, so that the exclusion still works if you sign up again.

You can download everything we hold about you as a file, and delete your account, from your account pages.
